## Build notes

- **The headline sets up a negative, so the copy resolves it fast.** "THIS IS NOT THERAPY" is arresting but tells you nothing about what Apricoti *is* — so the lead line lands immediately underneath, and the rule between them is there to mark the turn from claim to explanation.
- **"NOT" carries the accent** rather than "THERAPY". The word doing the work is the negation; highlighting "therapy" would have emphasised the thing you're not.
- **Type-led, not illustration-led.** A bold declarative headline wants space around it, so the illustration is deliberately small — enough to keep the warmth and the brand family, not enough to compete. The characters are the same pair from the young-member ad, at about half scale.
- **"Real people · not AI" sits in the header pill**, so the differentiator is stated before anyone reads the body copy. Worth keeping prominent — it's the point people will screenshot.
- **The CTA is plain text with an arrow**, not a button, since the image isn't clickable.
- Logo is inlined from your `public/icon.svg`, with the cleft highlight set to the page background so it blends rather than showing as white.

## Before you post

- **The disclaimer at the foot is doing real work.** "Not therapy, counselling or medical care" is the line that keeps a bold negative headline safe. Don't crop it out for a square version — rebuild the layout instead.
- **Expect this one to attract comments from therapists.** A headline defining yourself against therapy invites "so what is it then?" and occasionally "this is irresponsible". The body copy answers the first; for the second, have a considered reply ready that says Apricoti doesn't treat anything and points people to real support if they need it.
- **Don't let it drift into mental-health positioning.** The ad works because it's honest about being conversation, not care. If future creative starts implying it helps with anxiety or depression, the disclaimer stops protecting you.
- **Have a signposting policy live before this runs.** This framing will attract some people who are struggling. Companions need to know what to do when a conversation goes somewhere heavy, and there should be a route to real support in the product.
- **The £25 / 3 × 45-minute package must exist** and be priced exactly this way before the ad points at it.
