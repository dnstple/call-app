# Safeguarding Policy — DRAFT (solicitor / safeguarding review required)

> DRAFT for the closed pilot. Must be reviewed by a qualified safeguarding lead.

- **Principle:** the wellbeing of members — who may be older or vulnerable — comes
  first.
- **Companion boundaries:** professional, respectful conduct; no medical, legal or
  financial advice; never request passwords or financial credentials.
- **Reporting:** any user can report a concern from a conversation or booking.
  Support reviews safeguarding concerns; qualifying concerns hold any pending
  payout pending review.
- **Response:** suspend companions where warranted; preserve records; involve
  authorities where a person is at risk; contact emergency services in
  immediate danger.
- **Escalation & training:** [To be completed — named safeguarding lead,
  escalation path, record-keeping, staff training.]
