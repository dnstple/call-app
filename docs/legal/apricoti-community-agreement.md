# Apricoti Community Agreement

_Version 2 — 2026 — DRAFT for solicitor review._

## 1. Introduction and acceptance

This Agreement is between you and Apricoti Ltd ("Apricoti", "we", "us"). It sets out the terms on which you may use the Apricoti service, our safeguarding rules, how we handle personal data, the rules of our introduction reward programme, and how to raise a concern or complaint.
By scrolling to the end and confirming, you agree to be bound by this Agreement. If you are a Coordinator confirming on behalf of a Member, you confirm you have that person’s informed agreement to do so. If you do not agree, you should not use the service.
This is a single combined document covering all roles. Some sections apply only to specific roles (Members, Coordinators or Companions); those are marked.

## 2. What Apricoti is — and is not

Apricoti is a social-companionship service. It helps people arrange regular, friendly video conversations with a Companion they choose.
Apricoti is NOT a healthcare, medical, nursing, counselling, therapy, crisis or emergency service. Companions are not doctors, nurses, therapists, social workers or care professionals acting in that capacity, and must not give medical, legal or financial advice.
If you or someone you care for is in danger or needs urgent help, contact the emergency services on 999, or NHS 111 for urgent non-emergency medical help. Apricoti cannot respond to emergencies.
Conversations are not recorded by Apricoti and must not be recorded by participants.

## 3. Roles and eligibility

You must be 18 or over to hold an Apricoti account. The service is intended for use in the United Kingdom.
- Members are the people who have the conversations. A Member may use Apricoti themselves or be supported by a Coordinator.
- Coordinators are a trusted family member or friend who arranges and manages companionship on a Member’s behalf, with the Member’s consent. A Coordinator must never share the Member’s passwords, banking or card details, and must act in the Member’s best interests.
- Companions are vetted people who offer paid conversations, set their availability, and (for standard conversations) their own prices. Companions must complete identity verification before going live.
You are responsible for keeping your login secure and for activity on your account.

## 4. Safeguarding policy

Many people who use Apricoti may be older or otherwise vulnerable. Everyone in the community shares responsibility for keeping people safe. This section applies to all roles, and especially to Companions and Coordinators.
Boundaries and conduct. Companions must be kind, respectful and professional at all times. The following are strictly prohibited:
- Any form of abuse — physical, emotional, sexual, financial or discriminatory.
- Requesting, arranging or accepting money, gifts, loans, inheritance, or access to a Member’s finances, cards, bank accounts or passwords, outside Apricoti’s own payment system.
- Arranging to meet a Member in person, or moving the relationship off the platform.
- Giving medical, legal, financial or care advice, or holding oneself out as a professional in those fields.
- Recording, screenshotting, storing or sharing any conversation or a Member’s personal information.
- Romantic or sexual conduct, grooming, coercion, or exploiting a person’s loneliness, confusion or dependency.
Recognising and reporting concerns. If you become aware that someone may be at risk of harm, abuse or neglect, you must report it immediately using the in-app reporting tools, and where there is an immediate risk to life, contact 999. Apricoti will act on safeguarding reports, may suspend accounts pending review, and may share information with the police or the relevant local authority safeguarding team where necessary to protect a person at risk.
Verification. Companions complete an identity verification step (including a short identity video) before going live. Apricoti may carry out additional checks. Verification does not guarantee any individual’s conduct, and does not replace your own judgement.
Duty to cooperate. All users must cooperate with any safeguarding investigation and provide truthful information.

## 5. Companion obligations (Companions only)

As a Companion you agree to: keep to your stated availability or give reasonable notice; conduct conversations yourself (no substitutes); maintain a private, appropriate setting for calls; not be under the influence of alcohol or drugs during calls; and comply with all safeguarding rules above.
You are an independent provider, not an employee, worker or agent of Apricoti. You are responsible for your own tax and for declaring your Apricoti earnings to HMRC.
You must hold the right to work and to provide these services, and you must tell us promptly if anything changes that would affect your suitability (for example, a relevant caution or conviction, or a safeguarding concern raised elsewhere).

## 6. Coordinator authority and Member consent (Coordinators only)

As a Coordinator you confirm that the Member you support understands what Apricoti is and has agreed to you arranging conversations for them. You must act only in the Member’s interests and respect their wishes.
You must not impersonate the Member, misrepresent their views, or use the service in a way they would not want. The Member may withdraw their consent at any time, and you must stop acting for them if they do.
You must never share the Member’s passwords, PINs, card or bank details with Apricoti, a Companion, or anyone else.

## 7. Payments, prices, payouts and refunds

Trial conversations are a fixed 30 minutes for £5. Standard conversation prices are set by the Companion and shown before you book. You pay the price shown; Apricoti deducts the payment processor’s fee and its commission, and the Companion receives the remainder.
Payments are processed by Stripe. Companion payouts are made to the Companion’s connected Stripe account. Apricoti does not store your full card details.
Refunds, cancellations and disputes are handled in line with our in-app processes. Trial, cancelled, refunded, disputed or unpaid conversations may not count towards any reward or milestone.
You authorise Apricoti to take payment for conversations you book, and (for Companions) you authorise payouts of amounts genuinely earned, subject to holds where a concern, dispute or review is open.

## 8. Introduction reward programme

From time to time Apricoti runs an introduction reward programme. Where it applies:
- You may introduce a new Coordinator or Member by sharing your personal referral link. Do not send us anyone’s personal details — the person must register themselves or give appropriate consent.
- You receive £5 for a successful referral: once the household you introduced completes four paid calls, you receive £5. One £5 reward per qualifying household.
- Trial, cancelled, refunded, disputed, fraudulent or unpaid calls do not count. The person must be a genuinely new user and household. Existing users and self-referrals do not qualify. Only one referrer is attributed per household, and one reward is available per household.
- The pilot programme is limited to the first 25 qualifying households. Apricoti may review, pause or end the programme at any time, while honouring rewards already validly earned.
The reward is added to the referrer’s normal Apricoti payout or account credit, depending on account type.

## 9. Professional carers and public-sector staff

If you work professionally as a carer or support worker, you may take part only where this is permitted by your employer, your contract and your professional and regulatory policies. It is your responsibility to check and comply.
Apricoti does NOT offer or pay a personal financial incentive to NHS staff, social prescribers, local-authority social-care staff or other public-sector professionals in return for referring or introducing the people they support. Do not seek or accept an introduction reward in the course of your public-sector duties. Any such arrangement is only possible where a separate, formally approved agreement exists with the relevant organisation.
You must never use your professional position to pressure or unduly influence a person you support into joining Apricoti.

## 10. Data protection and privacy — who we are

Apricoti Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through the service. We process personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
This section explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and your rights. Questions about data protection can be sent to info@apricoti.co.uk.

## 11. What we collect and why (lawful bases)

We collect: account and profile details (name, preferred name, email, role, country, languages, interests, and — for Companions — profile and pricing information); booking, conversation, payment and payout records; messages and system notifications; support and safeguarding reports; verification information including a short Companion identity video; and technical data needed to run the service and video calls.
Our lawful bases are: performance of our contract with you (to provide the service you have asked for); our legitimate interests (to run, secure and improve the service, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep people safe); legal obligation (for example, financial and tax records, and safeguarding duties); and consent where required (for example, non-essential community and programme emails).
We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising by third parties.

## 12. Companion verification videos

Companion identity videos are stored in a private, access-controlled location. Only the Companion who recorded the video and Apricoti’s support team can access it, for the sole purpose of verifying identity.
Verification videos are permanently deleted once verification is complete (whether approved or rejected). They are not used for any other purpose, are not shared with Members or Coordinators, and are not used to train any automated system.

## 13. How long we keep data (retention)

- Verification videos: deleted on completion of verification.
- Account and profile data: kept while your account is active, and deleted or anonymised after account closure, subject to the records below.
- Financial records (payments, payouts, refunds, tax): kept for at least six years as required by law.
- Safeguarding and trust-and-safety records: kept as long as necessary to protect people and meet our legal duties.
- Messages and conversation metadata: kept for a limited period to support safety and dispute resolution, then deleted or anonymised.
Where you ask us to delete your data, we will do so unless we are required or permitted by law to keep specific records (for example financial or safeguarding records).

## 14. Who we share data with

We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf under contract: Stripe (payments and payouts), LiveKit (video calls), Supabase (secure database and storage hosting), Vercel (application hosting) and Resend (email delivery). They may process data outside the UK; where they do, appropriate safeguards (such as UK/EU approved transfer mechanisms) are in place.
We may share information with the police, a local authority or other authorities where necessary to protect a person at risk, to comply with the law, or to prevent or detect crime.
We do not share your personal details with other users beyond what is necessary for a conversation to take place, and Members’ private details are not disclosed to Companions except the minimum needed.

## 15. Your data protection rights

You have the right to access your data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data deleted in certain circumstances, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to data portability. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.
To exercise any right, contact info@apricoti.co.uk. We will respond within the time limits set by law. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, though we ask that you contact us first so we can try to put things right.
Security. We protect data with access controls, encryption in transit, row-level database security and least-privilege access. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will notify you and the ICO of a personal data breach where the law requires.

## 16. Acceptable use

You must not misuse the service. In particular you must not: harass, abuse, deceive or exploit anyone; impersonate another person; share unlawful, harmful or discriminatory content; attempt to take a relationship or payment off-platform; circumvent fees; attempt to access accounts or data that are not yours; or use the service for anything unlawful.
We may remove content, and suspend or close accounts, that breach this Agreement or put people at risk.

## 17. Suspension and termination

You may stop using Apricoti and close your account at any time. We may suspend or end your access — immediately where necessary to protect people — if you breach this Agreement, if there is a safeguarding or fraud concern, or if we are required to by law.
Ending your account does not remove records we must keep (for example financial or safeguarding records) as described in the retention section.

Nothing in this Agreement limits liability that cannot be limited by law (including for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud). Subject to that, we are not liable for losses that are not reasonably foreseeable or that arise from your breach of this Agreement.
This Agreement does not affect a consumer’s statutory rights.

## 19. Support and complaints

If something goes wrong, we want to know. For everyday help, use the in-app support or contact us at info@apricoti.co.uk.
To make a complaint: email info@apricoti.co.uk with the heading “Complaint”, telling us what happened, when, who was involved (if known), and what you would like us to do. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 5 working days and to give a full response within 20 working days; if we need longer we will tell you why.
Safeguarding concerns are prioritised and may be escalated immediately. If a person is in immediate danger, call 999 first.
If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to refer the matter to an appropriate body (for data-protection issues, the ICO; for other issues, a relevant consumer or ombudsman service where applicable).
