# Terms of Service — DRAFT (solicitor review required)

> DRAFT for the closed pilot. Not legal advice. Must be reviewed and completed by
> a qualified solicitor before any public launch.

1. **The service.** A social-companionship platform connecting members (and their
   coordinators) with companions for conversation. It is **not** healthcare,
   counselling, crisis or emergency support.
2. **Eligibility.** Adults (18+). Coordinators act with authority for the member
   they manage and confirm the member understands or agreed to the arrangement.
3. **Conduct.** Users must behave respectfully. Harassment, abuse, fraud and
   sharing of others' credentials are prohibited and may lead to suspension.
4. **Companions.** Companions are reviewed and approved before becoming
   discoverable and must not provide medical, legal or financial advice.
5. **Calls & privacy.** Calls are not recorded by the platform. See the Privacy
   Policy for data handling.
6. **Payments.** Fees, commission and refunds are described at booking. [Pilot:
   Stripe test mode.]
7. **Reporting & safety.** Concerns can be reported in-app and are reviewed by
   support. Emergencies must go to local emergency services.
8. **Liability, disputes, governing law.** [To be completed by solicitor.]
